About Kota Town

Kota Town is a Johannesburg-based street food brand celebrating South African Kota culture. We provide affordable, loaded Kotars with fresh ingredients and bold flavours for students, workers, and the late-night crowd.

Our mission is to deliver big portions, real township taste, and a community vibe in every bite. We offer loaded Kotars, combo meals, delivery, catering, and late-night service, with a vision of becoming a favourite late-night food spot and a home of authentic Kota culture.
